# Six-Part AI Prompting

**Six-Part Prompting** is a framework to create highly effective AI prompts for professional, tailored outputs. It guides the AI through:

1. ***Command***: Clear, actionable directive.  
2. ***Context***: Background, constraints, and goals.  
3. ***Logic***: Define output structure and format.  
4. ***Roleplay***: Assign AI an expert persona.  
5. ***Formatting***: Organize output for clarity and usability.  
6. ***Questions***: AI asks follow-up questions to refine results.

## Example: SaaS Business Plan

```text
1. COMMAND
Create a complete, professionally structured business plan.

2. CONTEXT
The business is a SaaS productivity tool for freelancers and small teams.
Features: task management, project tracking, time-logging, reporting.
Target: freelancers, remote teams, startups (Age 20–40)
Goal: Launch in 6 months, acquire 1,000 paid users in year one
Budget: Limited, lean model

3. LOGIC
Structure:
- Executive Summary
- Problem & Solution Overview
- Target Market & Customer Segments
- Product Description & Core Features
- Business Model & Revenue Strategy
- Marketing & Go-To-Market Plan
- Operations & Team Structure
- Financial Projections (1-year)
- Risk Analysis & Mitigation Plan
- Launch Timeline (6 months)

4. ROLEPLAY
You are a senior business strategist with 12+ years experience in SaaS startups. Use an analytical, investor-ready tone.

5. FORMATTING
- Clear headings
- Bullet points for insights
- Tables for pricing/projections
- Short paragraphs
- Month-by-month timeline
- Bold key insights
- Neat table for market segmentation

6. QUESTIONS
Ask 10 questions to tailor the business plan further: product features, pricing, target industry, competition, financial constraints.
